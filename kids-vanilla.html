Kids Vanilla

The exact vanilla base that became the house favorite.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 4 tbsp monk fruit + allulose blend
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 tbsp vanilla instant pudding mix
  • 1/4 tsp xanthan gum

Method

  1. Blend everything in a Vitamix for 10–15 seconds.
  2. Pour into the Ninja Creami Deluxe pint up to, but not over, the MAX FILL line.
  3. Freeze flat until completely solid.
  4. Run Ice Cream.
  5. If crumbly, add about 1 tbsp whole milk and Re-Spin.
  6. Add Mini M&M’s or crushed Oreos and run Mix-In.

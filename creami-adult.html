Adult / High-Protein Creami

Higher-protein and lighter Deluxe recipes. Freeze flat until completely solid; Lite Ice Cream unless noted. If crumbly, add 1 tbsp milk and Re-Spin.

Chocolate Peanut Butter

  • 1 Fairlife Nutrition Plan Chocolate shake (11.5 oz)
  • ½ cup whole milk
  • ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp PBfit
  • 1 tbsp Dutch cocoa
  • 1 tbsp natural peanut butter
  • 2 tbsp monk fruit + allulose
  • ⅛ tsp xanthan gum
  • ½ tsp vanilla
  • Pinch salt

Strawberry Cheesecake

  • 1 Fairlife Vanilla shake (11.5 oz)
  • 1 cup Greek yogurt
  • 150 g strawberries
  • 1 tbsp allulose
  • ¼ tsp xanthan gum
  • Pinch salt

Process on Frozen Yogurt; Re-Spin if needed.

Mocha Coffee

Vanilla Bean

Chocolate-Covered Strawberry

Peanut Butter Cheesecake

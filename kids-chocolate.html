Kids Chocolate

Creamy chocolate built from the same base as Kids Vanilla.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 4 tbsp monk fruit + allulose blend
  • 2 tbsp Dutch-process cocoa powder
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 tbsp chocolate instant pudding mix
  • 1/4 tsp xanthan gum
  • Small pinch of salt

Method

  1. Blend everything in a Vitamix for 10–15 seconds.
  2. Pour into the Ninja Creami Deluxe pint up to, but not over, the MAX FILL line.
  3. Freeze flat until completely solid.
  4. Run Ice Cream.
  5. If crumbly, add about 1 tbsp whole milk and Re-Spin.
  6. Add Mini M&M’s, Oreos, or mini chocolate chips and run Mix-In.

Crock-Pot Mexican Shredded Beef

Smoky, tender chuck roast for tacos, burrito bowls, quesadillas and freezer meal prep.

Ingredients

  • 3–4 lb chuck roast
  • 2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tsp chili powder
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tbsp avocado oil
  • 1 large red or yellow onion, sliced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • ½ cup beef broth
  • 1 can diced tomatoes or Rotel (14–15 oz)
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 1 chipotle pepper in adobo, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp adobo sauce
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce, optional

Method

  1. Pat the chuck roast dry. Mix salt, pepper, chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika and oregano, then rub all over the roast.
  2. Heat a cast-iron skillet over medium-high for 4–5 minutes. Add avocado oil and sear the roast 3–4 minutes per side. Sear thick edges for about 1 minute. Transfer to the crock pot.
  3. Turn skillet to medium. Add onion and cook 3–4 minutes. Add garlic and tomato paste and cook 30–60 seconds.
  4. Pour in ½ cup beef broth and scrape up all browned bits. Pour everything from the skillet into the crock pot.
  5. Add diced tomatoes/Rotel, lime juice, chipotle, adobo sauce and Worcestershire around the roast. Stir the liquid around the beef.
  6. Cook on LOW. If your crock pot runs hot, start checking at 5–6 hours. It is done when a fork twists through the thickest part with almost no resistance and the meat shreds easily. Chuck is often around 195–205°F when ready.
  7. Transfer beef to a board or bowl and shred with two forks. Remove large pieces of unrendered fat.
  8. Skim excess fat from the crock-pot liquid if needed. Return shredded beef to the juices and let sit on LOW/WARM 10–15 minutes before serving.

Serving

Excellent with tortillas, cotija, pico de gallo, cilantro, avocado, lime and cilantro-lime crema. For freezing, portion with some cooking liquid so the beef stays juicy when reheated.

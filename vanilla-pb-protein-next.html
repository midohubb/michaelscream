Vanilla PB Protein

Simple high-protein vanilla-PB base to try next.

Ingredients

  • 1 Fairlife Nutrition Plan Vanilla shake
  • ¾ cup whole milk
  • 1½ tbsp sugar-free vanilla instant pudding mix
  • 2 tbsp PBfit
  • 1½ tbsp monk fruit + allulose blend
  • 1 scoop vanilla protein powder
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ⅛ tsp salt

Method

  1. Blend everything in the Vitamix for 10–15 seconds.
  2. Pour into the Deluxe pint and freeze flat until completely solid.
  3. Before spinning, pour 1 tbsp whole milk over the frozen base.
  4. Run Lite Ice Cream.
  5. Add Reese's, Oreos, cookie dough, or another mix-in and run Mix-In.

Easy Variations

  • PB Cup: add chopped Reese's.
  • PB Oreo: add 2 crushed Oreos.
  • Cookies & Cream: leave out PBfit and add Oreos.

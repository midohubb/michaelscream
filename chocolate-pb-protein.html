Chocolate PB Protein

Rich chocolate-peanut-butter protein Creami with a thicker, sweeter base.

Ingredients

  • 1 Fairlife Nutrition Plan Chocolate shake (11.5 oz)
  • 1/2 cup whole milk
  • 1/4 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp PBfit
  • 1 tbsp Dutch-process cocoa powder
  • 1 tbsp natural peanut butter
  • 2 tbsp monk fruit + allulose blend
  • 1/8 tsp xanthan gum
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • Small pinch of salt
  • Optional: 1/2 tsp instant espresso powder
  • Optional: 1 scoop collagen peptides

Method

  1. Blend everything in a Vitamix for 10–15 seconds.
  2. Pour into the Deluxe pint and freeze flat until completely solid.
  3. Let sit 5–10 minutes if desired, then run Lite Ice Cream.
  4. If crumbly, add about 1 tbsp whole milk and Re-Spin, or stir vigorously by hand.

Skirt Steak Fajitas

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb skirt steak
  • ¼ cup fresh lime juice (about 2 limes)
  • 2 tbsp avocado oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tsp chili powder
  • 1½ tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ½ tsp dried oregano
  • ¼ tsp cayenne, optional
  • 3 bell peppers, sliced
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 1 tbsp avocado oil for vegetables
  • ½ tsp kosher salt, ½ tsp chili powder, ¼ tsp cumin for vegetables

Method

  1. Mix lime juice, 2 tbsp oil, garlic and seasonings; marinate steak 30 minutes–2 hours.
  2. Pat off excess marinade. Preheat cast iron over medium-high/high 5 minutes.
  3. Add a little avocado oil and sear steak 2–4 minutes per side; pull around 125–130°F for medium-rare or 135°F for medium.
  4. Rest 8–10 minutes. Cook peppers and onions in the same pan 6–8 minutes.
  5. Slice steak thinly against the grain and serve with tortillas, pico, cotija, cilantro, avocado, lime and crema.

Joe's Special — 2 lb Batch

Ingredients

  • 2 lb 88/12 ground beef
  • 8 large eggs
  • 16 oz fresh baby spinach, roughly chopped
  • 1 large yellow onion, diced
  • 8–16 oz mushrooms, sliced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp + 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1¼ tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • ¾ tsp garlic powder
  • ¼–½ tsp red pepper flakes
  • ⅓ cup grated Parmesan, optional

Method

  1. Brown beef 7–9 minutes; remove, leaving 1–2 tbsp fat.
  2. Cook onion and mushrooms 6–8 minutes. Add garlic for 30–60 seconds.
  3. Add spinach by handfuls until wilted; cook 2–3 minutes to evaporate moisture.
  4. Return beef and add Worcestershire and seasonings.
  5. Beat eggs, pour in, and gently fold over medium-low 3–5 minutes until just set.
  6. Fold in Parmesan if using.

¼-lb Smash Burger

Ingredients per burger

  • 4 oz ground beef (88/12 works well)
  • Kosher salt
  • Black pepper
  • 1 burger bun
  • 1–2 slices American cheese

Method

  1. Heat cast iron to about 450–500°F.
  2. Place patty in pan, cover with parchment, and smash once to about ¼ inch thick.
  3. Season and cook 90–120 seconds without moving.
  4. Scrape underneath, flip, add cheese, and cook 30–60 seconds.
  5. Toast bun and assemble.

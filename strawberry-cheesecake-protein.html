Strawberry Cheesecake Protein

A lighter cheesecake-style pint with berries, yogurt, and cream cheese.

Ingredients

  • 1 Fairlife Nutrition Plan Vanilla shake
  • 1/2 cup whole milk
  • 1/4 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 cup frozen strawberries
  • 1 oz cream cheese (2 tbsp)
  • 2 tbsp monk fruit + allulose blend
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/8 tsp xanthan gum
  • Tiny pinch of salt
  • Optional mix-in: 1 crushed graham cracker sheet

Method

  1. Blend everything except the graham cracker in a Vitamix for 15–20 seconds.
  2. Pour into the Deluxe pint and freeze flat until completely solid.
  3. Run Lite Ice Cream.
  4. If needed, add about 1 tbsp whole milk and Re-Spin.
  5. For cheesecake crunch, add the graham cracker after spinning and run Mix-In.

Savory Air-Fryer Sweet Potatoes

For 1.3 lb / ~590 g sweet potatoes

  • 1.3 lb sweet potatoes, ½-inch cubes
  • 1 tbsp avocado oil
  • 1¼ tsp kosher salt
  • ¾ tsp garlic powder
  • ¾ tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • Maldon flaky salt, optional finish
  1. Preheat air fryer to 400°F for 3–5 minutes.
  2. Toss potatoes with oil, then seasonings.
  3. Air fry at 400°F for 18–22 minutes, shaking at roughly 6, 12 and 16–18 minutes.
  4. Cook until browned outside and tender inside. Finish with a small pinch of Maldon.

Cilantro-Lime Greek Yogurt Crema

  • ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp fresh lime juice
  • ½ tsp lime zest, optional
  • ¼ tsp garlic powder
  • ¼ tsp kosher salt
  • 1–2 tbsp fresh cilantro
  • 1–2 tbsp water, as needed
  1. Add yogurt, lime, garlic powder, salt and cilantro to a small processor/blender.
  2. Blend smooth.
  3. Add water 1 tsp at a time until drizzleable. Taste and add up to another ½ tbsp lime juice for more punch.

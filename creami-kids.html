Kid-Friendly Creami

Freeze flat until completely solid. Use the listed mode and Re-Spin with a splash of milk if needed.

Kids Vanilla — House Recipe

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 4 tbsp monk fruit + allulose blend
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 tbsp vanilla instant pudding mix
  • ¼ tsp xanthan gum
  1. Blend 10–15 seconds.
  2. Pour only to the Deluxe MAX FILL line and freeze flat.
  3. Process on Ice Cream.

Kids Chocolate

  • 1½ cups whole milk
  • ½ cup heavy cream
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • ¼ cup sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • Pinch salt

Process on Ice Cream.

Strawberry Frozen Yogurt

  • 1 cup whole milk
  • ¾ cup Greek yogurt
  • 175 g strawberries
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • ½ tsp vanilla
  • Pinch salt

Process on Frozen Yogurt. Add 1 tbsp milk and Re-Spin if needed.

Cookies & Cream

  • 1½ cups whole milk
  • ½ cup heavy cream
  • ¼ cup sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 2 crushed Oreos after first spin

Ice Cream, then Mix-In with Oreos.

Banana Cream

  • 2 ripe bananas
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • ½ cup Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp honey

Process on Frozen Yogurt.

Strawberry Banana

  • 1 banana
  • 150 g strawberries
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • ½ cup Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp honey

Process on Frozen Yogurt.

Mango Sorbet

  • 2 cups mango
  • ¼ cup orange juice
  • 1 tbsp honey

Blend before freezing so there are no loose hard fruit pieces. Process on Sorbet.

Watermelon Sorbet

  • 2 cups watermelon
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tbsp honey

Blend before freezing. Process on Sorbet.

Peach Frozen Yogurt

  • 175 g peaches
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • ¾ cup Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp honey

Blend before freezing. Process on Frozen Yogurt.

Chocolate Banana

  • 1 banana
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • ½ cup Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1 tbsp honey

Process on Frozen Yogurt.

Birthday Cake / Vanilla Party

  • 1½ cups whole milk
  • ½ cup heavy cream
  • ¼ cup sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 tbsp sprinkles after first spin

Ice Cream, then Mix-In with sprinkles.
